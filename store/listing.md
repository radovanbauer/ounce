# Play Store listing

**App name (30):** Ounce: Calorie Tracker

**Short description (80):** Snap a meal, get calories & macros. Private, no account, on-device AI.

**Full description (4000):**

Snap a photo of your meal and Ounce estimates the foods, portions, calories, protein, carbs and fat, then logs it for you. Got something wrong? Tap to adjust a portion, or just type "only half the rice" or "no dressing".

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
• Your food log stays on your phone (and in your own Android backup).
• On supported phones, photos are analyzed by on-device AI (Gemini Nano), so they never leave your phone.

LOG IN SECONDS
• One-tap camera: snap and you're done; analysis happens in the background.
• Packaged food? Point the camera at the barcode and Ounce looks it up in Open Food Facts.
• Prefer typing? "Two eggs, toast with butter and a latte" works too.
• Home-screen widget with calories left, plus shortcuts to snap or type.

SHARPER ESTIMATES WITH YOUR OWN AI KEY (OPTIONAL)
Use your own API key for Google Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or OpenRouter. Pick any model from the provider's live list. You pay the provider directly, typically a fraction of a cent per meal.

TRACK PROGRESS
• Daily calorie and macro goals
• Weight tracking with trend line
• Calorie and weight charts
• Export to CSV or Google Sheets

OPEN SOURCE
Ounce is free and open source (MIT): github.com/radovanbauer/ounce

Calorie estimates from photos are approximate. Ounce is not a medical device and doesn't provide medical advice.
