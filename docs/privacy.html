Ounce

Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Ounce is a calorie and nutrition tracker for Android made by Radovan Bauer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: Ounce has no account and no server. We don't collect, receive or sell any of your data. Data only leaves your phone when you use a feature that needs an outside service, and then it goes directly from your phone to that service.

Data stored on your phone

Your food log (meals, foods, portions, nutrition values, times and photos), body weight entries, goals and settings are stored only on your device. If you've turned on Android backup, your food log, weights, goals and settings are included in your device's backup to your Google account, which Google manages; meal photos and API keys are excluded. Uninstalling Ounce deletes everything stored by the app.

When data leaves your phone

  • On-device AI (default). On supported phones, meals are analyzed by Gemini Nano running on your device through Android's AICore. Photos and descriptions are not sent anywhere.
  • Cloud AI (optional, your own API key). If you add an API key for Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or OpenRouter, the meal photo or description you're logging, plus a correction you type, is sent from your phone directly to that provider to estimate nutrition. That provider processes it under its own terms and privacy policy and the agreement you have with them. Your API key is stored encrypted on your phone and is never included in backups.
  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a packaged food, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product. Nothing else is sent.
  • Google ML Kit diagnostics. Barcode scanning and on-device AI use Google's ML Kit, which sends Google technical diagnostics (device and app information, performance metrics, error codes and installation identifiers) to keep those features working. It doesn't include your photos, meals or barcode contents. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
  • Export. CSV export only happens when you share the files, to the destination you choose. Google Sheets export is described below.

Google user data (Google Sheets export)

If you choose "Export to Google Sheets", Ounce asks for permission to the Google Drive scope drive.file, which only lets the app see and edit files it creates itself, not any other files in your Drive. Ounce uses this access solely to create a spreadsheet named "Ounce" in your Drive and to write your exported food log, daily totals and weights to it when you tap sync. The access token is used only on your device; your Google data isn't sent to us or anyone else, and isn't used for advertising or any other purpose. You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Ounce's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph meals and read barcodes, only while the camera screen is open.
  • Internet: only for the optional services above.

What we don't do

No analytics of our own, no ads, no tracking, no data sales. We don't operate servers that receive your data.

Children

Ounce is intended for adults (18 and over). Using your own Gemini API key requires being 18 or older under Google's terms.

Health information

Nutrition estimates are approximate. Ounce is not a medical device and doesn't provide medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: ounce@radovanbauer.com or GitHub issues.
